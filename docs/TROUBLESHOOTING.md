# Troubleshooting

## The side panel does not appear

- Confirm you are on a product detail page containing a valid 10-character ASIN.
- Confirm the store is `amazon.com`, `amazon.co.uk`, `amazon.ae`, or `amazon.sa`.
- Open the extension settings and enable **Show product-page side widget**.
- Reload the extension from the extensions page, then refresh the Amazon tab.

## Official fees are unavailable

- Confirm Amazon displays a valid selling price or enter one in the full panel.
- Open the Amazon Calculator button and check whether Amazon recognizes the ASIN there.
- Amazon may require cookies, reject automated requests, throttle the browser, or temporarily change its Revenue Calculator interface.
- The extension intentionally does not replace a failed official response with a guessed fee.

## The Buy Box price is missing or incorrect

- Select the intended variation and wait for the page to finish updating.
- Check whether Amazon shows a range, coupon-only price, business price, used offer, or unavailable Buy Box.
- Enter the intended selling price manually and refresh official fees.

## Seller counts differ from another tool

The extension reports unique offers returned to the current browser. Delivery location, sign-in state, pagination, suppression, stock, condition, and Amazon experiments can change that sample. It is not labelled a total marketplace count.

## BSR, dimensions, or weight are missing

Amazon does not expose every field for every ASIN and may use different page layouts. Missing fields remain unavailable. Check the product details section and the official calculator before making a purchase decision.

## Settings did not update an already open page

Refresh the Amazon tab after saving settings. Manually installed extension updates also require pressing reload on the browser's extension card.
